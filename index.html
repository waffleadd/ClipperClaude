ClipperClaude

Effective date: November 24, 2026

ClipperClaude is a personal desktop application for Windows. It turns long videos into short vertical clips and, when the user asks it to, uploads those clips to the user's own TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts through each platform's official API. It is not a web service: it runs only on the user's computer and has no servers of its own.

How it works

  1. You choose a long video from your computer.
  2. The app transcribes it locally and Claude suggests the best moments.
  3. It creates vertical 9:16 clips with subtitles and on-screen text.
  4. When you click Upload, it publishes them to your own TikTok, Instagram or YouTube account through the official APIs.

ClipperClaude is an independent tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok, Meta, Google or Anthropic.
