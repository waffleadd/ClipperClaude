ClipperClaude Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

ClipperClaudePrivacy PolicyTerms of Service

Summary

ClipperClaude does not collect, sell or share personal data with its developer or with any third party for advertising. Everything it stores stays on the user's own computer.

What data the app handles and why

DataPurposeWhere it is kept
TikTok access and refresh tokens, and the account's open ID (scopes user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish)To upload the user's videos to the user's own TikTok account when the user clicks UploadA local file on the user's computer (token_tiktok.json)
Instagram and Facebook Page access tokens and the Instagram account IDTo publish Reels to the user's own Instagram accountA local file on the user's computer
YouTube OAuth tokensTo upload videos to the user's own YouTube channelA local file on the user's computer
Video files, titles, descriptions and hashtags chosen by the userTo create clips and send them to the platforms the user selectsThe user's computer, and the platform the user uploads to
Text transcripts of the videosSent to Anthropic's Claude to suggest which moments to clipThe user's computer; processed by Anthropic under the user's own Claude account

What the app does not do

  • It does not read the user's TikTok followers, messages, likes or other videos.
  • It does not post anything without the user starting the upload.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking, cookies or advertising identifiers.
  • It does not send any data to the app's developer.

Third-party services

Data sent to TikTok, Instagram (Meta), YouTube (Google) or Anthropic is handled under those companies' own privacy policies. ClipperClaude only sends what is needed to complete the action the user requested.

Retention and deletion

Tokens and files stay on the user's computer until the user deletes them. The user can delete the token files or the whole ClipperClaude folder at any time. Access can also be revoked from the platform itself; on TikTok, under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services permissions.

Children

ClipperClaude is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly process their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top of this page will be updated.

ClipperClaude is an independent tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok, Meta, Google or Anthropic.
